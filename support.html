Common Questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open the pause menu (or the main menu) and tap Restore Purchases. Apple/Google will verify your account and reinstate Premium.

How do I evolve into the Spartan?

Collect all the rings in a stage. A gem will appear — pick it up to transform.

What do the colored gems do?

Each color is a different power-up: blue laser, fast laser, ball laser, shield, armor, or rotisserie chicken for full health.
