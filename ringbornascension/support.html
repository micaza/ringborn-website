Common Questions

How do I unlock new ships?

Collect rings during gameplay. As you gather enough rings, new ships become available — each with its own weapon to help you push further.

What do the colored gems do?

Each color is a different power-up: blue laser, fast laser, ball laser, shield, armor, or rotisserie chicken for full health.

Does the game support controllers?

Yes. Ringborn Ascension supports controllers on both mobile and desktop, including Steam.
