The Game

In deep space, pilot Rebecca against the Spikes — hostile eye AI bent on taking her down. Collect rings to unlock new ships, each armed with a different weapon to push further into the fight. Grab power-ups mid-run, face the boss, and uncover Rebecca's story as you play.
