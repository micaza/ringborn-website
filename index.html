The Game

Drag to dodge. Collect rings. Grab the rifle to evolve into a battle-hardened Spartan with a powerful laser. Pick up power-ups, deflect spikes with your shield, and survive the endless ascent through stages of increasing danger.

Beyond stage 30 the enemies start firing homing missiles. By stage 55, you'll face both — the standard volleys and the missiles, at once. Good luck.

Features

🎯

Easy to Learn

One-finger touch control. Hold and drag to move and fire. Lift to stop shooting.

⚔️

Spartan Evolution

Collect the gem, transform, and unleash powerful lasers on the spikes.

💎

Six Power-Ups

Blue laser, fast laser, ball laser, shield, armor, and health.

🚀

Endless Stages

Every stage adds more rings, more spikes, and at higher stages — homing missiles.

Power-Ups

Health

Restores your full health bar.

Blue Laser

Triples your laser damage — punch through spikes in fewer shots.

Fast Laser

Quadruples your fire rate. Hold and watch the bullets fly.

Ball Laser

Fires a glowing orb that explodes on impact, launching eight lasers in every direction.

Shield

Five seconds of total invincibility. Run straight through anything.

Armor

Halves the damage you take from every hit. The hero glows blue while active.
