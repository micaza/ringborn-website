The Game

Touch and drag to pilot your spaceship. Collect rings, grab the gun to evolve into the armed ship — a battle-ready fighter that fires powerful lasers. Deflect spikes with your nose shield, pick up power-ups, and survive the endless ascent through stages of increasing danger.

The further you go, the harder it gets. Enemies grow in number, move faster, and evolve in ways you won't see coming. Every stage is a new test of reflexes and survival instinct. How far can you make it?

Features

🎯

Easy to Learn

One-finger touch control. Drag to fly, lift to stop shooting. Inertia-based flight gives every move a real sense of momentum.

✈️

It Feels Alive

Your ship doesn't just teleport — it accelerates toward your finger, boosting thruster particles as it picks up speed. Every maneuver feels like you're actually flying.

🎵

Sound & Music

An original soundtrack and punchy sound effects bring every clash, laser shot, and close call to life.

👾

Retro Soul, Modern Feel

Pixel art visuals and chunky effects nod to the classics — but fluid animations, dynamic shaders, and responsive controls make it feel right at home on a modern device.

🛸

Spartan Evolution

Collect the gun, transform your ship into the armed spaceship fighter, and unleash powerful lasers on incoming spikes.

🛡️

Nose Shield

Your hero ship's front shield deflects spikes on contact — watch the ripple effect and send them flying back.

🚀

Endless Stages

Every stage adds more rings, more spikes, and at higher stages — homing missiles that track your every move.
