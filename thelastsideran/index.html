The Game

In deep space, pilot Zenia against the Spikes — hostile eye AI bent on taking her down. Collect star essence to unlock new ships, each armed with a different weapon to push further into the fight, and reveal unlockable story as you progress. Grab power-ups mid-run and face the boss in fast-paced arcade action.
