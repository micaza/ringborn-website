Common Questions

How do I unlock new ships?

Collect star essence during gameplay. As you gather enough star essence, new ships become available — each with its own weapon to help you push further.

What do the colored gems do?

Each color is a different power-up: blue laser, fast laser, ball laser, shield, or armor.

Does the game support controllers?

Yes. The Last Sideran supports controllers on both mobile and desktop, including Steam.

Where can I play the demo?

A free demo is available on itch.io: pixeldogames.itch.io/the-last-sideran.

Where can I buy the game?

The Last Sideran is available now on Steam: store.steampowered.com. A free demo is on itch.io. iOS and Android are coming soon.
